# Three-seed transfer/no-transfer assessment and direction adjustment

## Input comparison

At tick 1500 the costed-transfer condition had 12,208–14,428 committed transfers and 2,066.55–2,243.77 effective transferred roots. The no-transfer condition correctly reported zero proposals, commits and transferred roots.

Three-seed endpoint means:

| condition | alive | effective lineages | strategy dimensions | action entropy | cohesion | affinity dimensions |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| no transfer | 1353.00 | 20.6045 | 18.4596 | 1.7389 | 0.4051 | 2.5387 |
| costed transfer | 1356.67 | 19.8283 | 17.5670 | 1.7372 | 0.3967 | 2.4633 |

The differences are small relative to seed-to-seed variation and their directions are not consistent for lineage concentration, strategy dimensions or cohesion. Therefore the 1500-tick data do not establish a robust global demographic effect of transfer.

## Direction adjustment

The spatially asynchronous environment can stabilize global population while maintaining local pressure heterogeneity. Global mortality/cohesion correlations are therefore an incomplete diagnostic. v0.19 shifts the next experiments to local region-window panels and later local checkpoint interventions.

Costed transfer should be evaluated through local information reach, transferred-root persistence, cross-region/group transitions and survival through local stress events rather than global population alone.
