# Subject Graph VM primary-direction decision

Decision ID: `subject-graph-vm-primary-direction-v1`
Release: `0.107.0`
Status: accepted

The primary subject-formation substrate is a partitioned unified subject graph. The project deliberately presets general cognitive architecture and initially biased computational regions to reduce evolutionary search time. It does not preset concrete cognition, subjective value, social concepts or role conclusions.

D1-X and D1-Y remain reproducible fixed-cognition comparison baselines and engineering fixtures. They are not extended as the primary subject model with additional named benefit ledgers.

The unified graph uses shared node and edge identities. Regions bias early development but may be crossed, resized, weakened or reassigned through later evolvable schemas. Interest-like long-term internal organization and decision may therefore overlap in one recurrent graph. Activation and delayed plasticity remain causally phase-ordered so a current action cannot validate itself before its physical outcome exists.
