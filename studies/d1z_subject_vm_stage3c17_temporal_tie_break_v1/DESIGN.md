# Design

Stage 3C-17 holds the reachable Stage-3C-16 `edge_forward_gate` carrier-on baseline fixed and changes only how exact normalized-dot similarity ties are resolved. The baseline prefers the latest eligible historical token; the alternative prefers the oldest eligible historical token. Candidate validity, delay bounds, similarity calculation, threshold, target family, eligibility carrier, delta, exposure, rollback and score-free evaluation remain fixed.

The comparison does not assign value to recency or age. It asks whether the observed delay-one collapse is an implementation-neutral consequence of the token geometry or a material fixed-bootstrap addressing bias. Neither arm authorizes permanent retention or a learning claim.
