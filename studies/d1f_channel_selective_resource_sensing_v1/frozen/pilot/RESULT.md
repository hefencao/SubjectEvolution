# D1-F pilot result

The three source checkpoints and both branches satisfy the registered shared
checkpoint, paired-randomness, genotype-preservation, resource-field, and
cost-preservation contracts.

Paired differences for the inherited affinity-routed branch minus the
cost-preserving radius-one branch were:

| seed | alive | births | deaths | mean energy |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 85001 | +11 | +1 | -10 | -0.019667 |
| 85002 | -20 | -10 | +10 | +0.014523 |
| 85003 | -17 | -19 | -2 | +0.013636 |

The median living-count difference is -17 and the median mean-energy difference
is +0.013636. This is substantially less uniformly negative than D1-E, but it
is not a repeated positive ecological result. At tick 480, affinity
specialization remains low (about 0.15–0.17) and the strongest-channel routing
concentrates the whole extra-radius budget into one channel even when inherited
resource use remains distributed across several channels.

The result therefore supports a carrier-capability revision, not a reward,
cost, threshold, or ecological interpretation change. D1-G keeps the same
inherited total reach and costs while distributing the fixed integer
extra-radius budget across channels by inherited affinity.
