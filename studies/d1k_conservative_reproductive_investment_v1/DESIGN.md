# D1-K conservative parental investment and generational substrate

## Why the existing sample is not evolutionary

D1-J uses three independent source seeds and exact shared-checkpoint branches.
That is sufficient for wiring and acute mechanism calibration. At tick 480,
however, the source populations contain only 1--8 cumulative births and are
dominated by founders. Additional nested ticks or more observations from the
same three trajectories cannot substitute for independent multi-generation
replacement.

## Capability

Morphology gene 6 selects one of four parent-to-offspring energy investments.
The parent pays a fixed event overhead plus the selected transfer and must retain
a configured post-event reserve. The transfer becomes newborn energy before
existing development costs. Larger investment therefore reduces reproductive
frequency while increasing offspring quality; no reward or role is assigned.
The configured levels have mean total parent debit equal to the legacy 1.4
energy cost, but make its previous hidden 45% newborn fraction and dissipation
explicit.

External same-channel resource recycling remains enabled because the D1-J source
also loses raw resources at death and store decay. Recycling closes that material
path but is not itself the paired estimand.

## Cost-preserving neutralization

`neutralize-conservative-offspring-endowment` keeps the inherited investment,
parent energy debit, eligibility requirement, fertility debit, genotype, stores,
resource fields, residue inventory, checkpoint, and random keys unchanged. It
removes only the newborn energy return by dissipating the already-paid transfer.

## Sample boundary

The first three-seed panel remains a substrate calibration. It can establish
conservation, target engagement, large offspring-quality effects, and whether
descendant turnover becomes measurable. Selection, adaptation, trait-frequency,
coexistence, and niche claims require a later independent panel whose source
trajectories already show repeated multi-generation replacement. No automatic
pass/fail gate changes a running seed.
