# D1-K conservative parental investment

This study makes reproduction investment and offspring quality an explicit,
inherited, costed subject capability. It also reports whether the source panel
contains enough generational turnover for a later evolutionary experiment.

```text
se-study show studies/d1k_conservative_reproductive_investment_v1
```

The pilot and paired branch are calibration only. They do not authorize
selection, adaptation, coexistence, or niche claims.
