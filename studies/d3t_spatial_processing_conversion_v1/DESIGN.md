# D3-T design

## Question

Does the existing phase-shifted local spatial-processing support surface have a
material direct effect on realized conservative raw-resource conversion?

## Intervention and cost boundary

The intervention neutralizes only spatial-processing support. It preserves the
conversion request, execution cost, genotype, resource fields, source
checkpoint and all unrelated mechanisms. No reward, role, sensor or population
protection is added.

## Estimand

The primary metric is cumulative `resource-converted-total` over 120 ticks.
The independent unit is the seed, the practical threshold is 2%, and target
engagement requires non-zero requested conversion plus non-neutral baseline
support exposure that is exactly removed by intervention.

## Stage contract

Screen, replication and confirmation use disjoint seed sets. Replication and
confirmation preserve the complete source protocol except for seed. Scale or
horizon changes are a separate robustness study, not a replication or
confirmation.
