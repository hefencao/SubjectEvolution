# Design

## Problem

D1-K reached tick 720 with only 12–19 living entities, mean generation below
0.6, and founder-dominated survivors. The later paired window is therefore not
an interpretable gene or evolution sample.

## Change in development order

1. Qualify a neutral demographic substrate without adding a new inherited capability.
2. Stop at tick 120 or 240 when population scale, turnover, descendant survival,
   or checkpoint-to-checkpoint stability fails.
3. Only after repeated substrate readiness may a low-cost capability be attached.
4. Combination-dependent capabilities require an explicit maturation window before
   effect measurement.

The gate is an execution precondition. It does not feed back into the world and
is not a pass/fail test of a gene.
