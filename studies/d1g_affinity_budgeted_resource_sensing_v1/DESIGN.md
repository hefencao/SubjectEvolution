# D1-G affinity-budgeted resource-sensing reach

## Charter role

D1-F showed that concentrating the complete extra-radius budget into one
strongest-affinity channel removes much of D1-E's uniform penalty, but does not
produce a repeated positive outcome. The source populations remain weakly
specialized and use several resource channels. A single winner-take-all sensing
channel is therefore a carrier-capability bottleneck rather than evidence that
broader sensing should be rewarded or made cheaper.

## Capability contract

Morphology gene 7 still chooses inherited total reach capacity from 1, 2, 4,
and 8. Each carrier starts with radius one on all four resource channels. The
remaining integer budget `capacity - 1` is apportioned across channels in
proportion to inherited fixed-budget resource affinity using deterministic
Hamilton apportionment and lowest-index tie breaking.

For every entity:

```text
sum(channel_radius - 1) == inherited_capacity - 1
```

Thus D1-G and D1-F have exactly the same total extra radius and the same
maintenance, use, and development costs for the same genotype. D1-G changes
only how that fixed information-scale budget is distributed.

## Calibration boundary

The first three-seed panel repeats the exact shared-checkpoint radius-one
neutralization for 120 ticks. It confirms that budget closure, multi-channel
allocation, costs, checkpoint identity, and world interaction are measurable.
There is no pass/fail threshold and no ecological, adaptation, coexistence, or
selection claim.
