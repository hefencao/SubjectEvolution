# Design

## Problem

D1-M produced two fully qualified tick-360 seeds. A third seed was stopped at
tick 240 because a 30.43% decline from an earlier population overshoot narrowly
missed a 30% trajectory target. At that point the population still equalled the
founder count and already contained substantial living descendants. The source
was not catastrophically collapsed; the execution contract was overly eager.

A separate implementation-calibration panel then showed that the D1-M throughput
remained insufficiently robust at tick 480. This calibration used seeds that are
excluded from formal evidence. The failure was addressed before preregistering
the formal D1-N seeds, not by reinterpreting a completed scientific panel.

## D1-N boundary

D1-N adds no inherited capability, reward, role, or population protection. It
keeps the fixed conservative non-heritable reproduction settlement and applies
one uniform substrate calibration to every entity:

- base maintenance: 0.011 -> 0.010 energy/tick;
- four-channel harvest multiplier: 1.25 -> 1.30;
- four-channel abiotic regeneration: 0.025 -> 0.027.

The health contract separates:

- advisory tick-120/240/360 qualification diagnostics;
- broad catastrophic floors that alone may terminate execution;
- one required tick-480 qualification that alone authorizes the next stage.

The final criteria require population scale, at least 0.85 cumulative births per
founder, at least 0.40 living descendants per founder, mean generation at least
0.65, founder fraction at most 0.50, and bounded final checkpoint decline. These
criteria and all physical parameters are fixed before seeds 93101--93103 run.

## Authorization

Three independent formal seeds must complete and pass the required tick-480
checkpoint. Warnings remain visible. A hard stop or final failure blocks
capability attachment, formal affordability budgeting, paired branches, and
evolutionary claims.
