# D1-J frozen pilot result

The three exact tick-480 shared-checkpoint pairs satisfy checkpoint identity,
paired randomness, genotype, current-store, fixed total storage, conversion,
physiology-cost, and resource-field contracts.

Inherited storage allocation changes channel-specific storage, conversion and
body flow, but the endpoint effects are small and mixed. Living-count
differences are 0, -1 and 0; mean-energy differences are +0.002020,
+0.021461 and -0.019991. Relative to uniform storage, effective allocation
dimensions change by -0.036866, -0.036814 and -0.047710.

The source populations are not evolutionary samples. At tick 480 the three
500-entity runs retain only 53, 55 and 46 living carriers, with cumulative
births of 8, 6 and 1. Mean living age is approximately 479, 458 and 480 ticks,
so most survivors are founders and the panel contains almost no generational
replacement.

D1-J is frozen as a valid acute substrate calibration only. Three seeds and a
120-tick paired horizon are adequate to verify wiring and detect large acute
mechanism effects, but not to infer selection, adaptation, stable ecological
specialization or long-horizon evolutionary consequences. Simply extending the
same over-dense founder configuration would mostly measure continued decline.
