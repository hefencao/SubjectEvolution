# D1-M marginal qualification

D1-M does not establish either substrate success or substrate failure. Seeds 92102
and 92103 completed tick 360 and passed every registered qualification condition.
Seed 92101 was stopped at tick 240 only because its population declined 30.43%
from the tick-120 overshoot, narrowly exceeding the 30% qualification target.
At that stop it still retained 128 living entities from 128 founders, had produced
98 births, retained 56 living descendants, and had mean generation 0.4375.

The failure is therefore an execution-gate design problem: a desired final
trajectory bound was also used as an immediate stop rule. No threshold is relaxed
post hoc and D1-M is not promoted. The unchanged non-heritable substrate must be
repeated on disjoint seeds under a staged contract that separates advisory
qualification misses from catastrophic runtime floors.
