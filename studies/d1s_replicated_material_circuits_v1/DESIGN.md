# D1-S design

## D1-R failure carried forward

The frozen D1-R formal report records three complete 1800-tick runs with
physical heterogeneity and internal raw-resource exchange in every seed. Only
two seeds reproduced multiple persistent division-candidate groups. Seed 97103
had a minimum population of 42, final effective-lineage count near 3.03 and a
largest-lineage fraction near 0.443. Its absence of replicated division is
therefore confounded by a deep demographic bottleneck and lineage
concentration.

The compact D1-R bundle did not retain each seed's group windows, environment
atlas and evolution-progress rows. D1-S freezes the available formal reports and
run summaries, treats the missing detail as a reviewability limitation, and
updates future result packaging so these observational files are included by
default.

## Shared physical change

D1-S changes two parameters only:

- antipodal resource-circuit weight: 0.35 to 0.55;
- uniform source/processing province radius scale: 1.00 to 1.15.

The spatial field for each channel is normalized after combining its primary
and antipodal circuit. Therefore the global mean material opportunity remains
unchanged. The change seeks multiple independently reachable material loops,
not more total resource.

No inherited coordinate, mutation rate, maintenance cost, reproduction rule,
recipe, exchange rule, group detector threshold, role label or fitness term is
changed.

## Qualification

Each formal seed must satisfy all existing D1-R requirements and:

- minimum recorded alive / initial population >= 0.50;
- final effective founder lineages >= 4.0;
- at least two persistent division-candidate group lineages;
- at least two candidate groups in the same observation window;
- real within-group raw-resource exchange.

All three independent seeds must pass. A single-seed probe never carries formal
evidence. Failure returns the project to shared-environment debugging and does
not authorize gene-level adjustment.
