# D1-S replicated material circuits and bottleneck-aware qualification

D1-R did not pass its preregistered environment-plurality threshold: seeds
97101 and 97102 each produced two persistent within-group division candidates,
while seed 97103 produced none that persisted across two windows. The failure is
formal and is not repaired by changing the division threshold.

D1-S keeps the complete subject, complementary recipes, raw-resource exchange,
processing offsets, costs and reproduction unchanged. It changes only the
shared physical opportunity geometry. Each resource-processing chain retains a
primary circuit and receives a stronger antipodal circuit, while all source and
processing provinces are widened uniformly. Per-channel spatial fields remain
normalized to the same global mean, so the change redistributes access rather
than adding total material or rewarding a group, lineage, genotype or role.

The formal gate remains at least two persistent division-candidate group
lineages in every independent seed. D1-S additionally rejects samples that fall
below half the initial population at any recorded point or finish with fewer
than four effective founder lineages. These demographic checks prevent a deep
bottleneck followed by expansion of a few lineages from being treated as
replicated social structure.

`environment-probe` is parameter debugging only. It may diagnose whether the
shared geometry is runnable, but it cannot qualify the environment or authorize
any gene audit. Only the complete three-seed `structured-panel` followed by
`structure-summary` can reach the environment threshold, and even a pass does
not authorize genetic, selection, adaptation or subjecthood claims in this
version.
