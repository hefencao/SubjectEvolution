# D1-Q integrated equilibrium and retention

D1-P confirmed that the complete 704-coordinate subject and plural environment
remain executable through several generations, but all three formal populations
were still expanding at tick 1200. Its absolute v1 persistence threshold therefore
reported every coordinate as retained while lineage concentration and active
expansion still confounded relative contraction.

D1-Q does **not** add a gene, create a gene-specific environment, or resume the
candidate ledger. It changes one role-neutral physical quantity shared by every
entity and every resource channel: external resource regeneration changes from
`0.027` to `0.00675`. Geometry, channel effects, capacity, reproduction, costs,
all genes, and all expression routes remain unchanged.

The frozen formal pilot seed 96032 passed both minimum health and a cycle-aware
bounded-regime gate at tick 840. The gate assesses tick 390--840, covering 450
ticks against the longest configured environmental forcing period of 431 ticks.
Population remained between 109 and 143, cycle-window CV was 0.0855, normalized
slope was 0.0148 per 30-tick sample, 19.68 effective founder lineages remained,
and the largest lineage fraction was 0.1049. A three-window rebound remains in
the report as advisory phase evidence; it does not override the full-cycle gate.

The frozen qualification authorizes only three new independent integrated-panel
seeds. It does not authorize a gene-specific adjustment, paired experiment,
selection estimate, or ecological-niche claim.

The executable order is declared only in `workflow.toml`: inspect the study, run `evidence-audit` and `prepare-config`, then execute `integrated-panel`, the two panel gates, the two retention analyses, and `pack-results`.


`source-pilot`, `pilot-health`, and `pilot-equilibrium` remain available for exact
reproduction of the frozen pilot. They are not required to rerun before the
