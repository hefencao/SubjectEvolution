# D1-Q integrated equilibrium — retired

D1-Q reduced uniform external resource renewal and froze one bounded pilot, but
the subsequently reported three-seed formal panel produced no passing seed.
The study is therefore retired as a failed environment/demographic construction,
not promoted into genetic interpretation.

The workflow retains config generation and exact demographic reproduction steps
for historical failure evidence. The former whole-genome persistence and
retention steps have been removed from the executable workflow. No D1-Q result
can authorize a coordinate adjustment, paired experiment, selection estimate,
ecological-niche claim or single-round audit.

D1-R supersedes this study by constructing plural source and processing
provinces, complementary material recipes, raw-resource exchange and
observational group-internal division diagnostics before any genetic study is
reconsidered.
