# D1-O design: bounded capability attachment after D1-N

## Qualified source

D1-N completed seeds 93101--93103 to tick 480 with one canonical
`source-health-contract-v2`. All required-final checks passed, no warning or
catastrophic hard-stop occurred, and living descendants formed 63.6%--70.1% of
the final populations. This authorizes capability budgeting and a new source
pilot, not an evolutionary claim.

## Budget

The frozen budget derives a conservative reference from the weakest formal
D1-N throughput and reserve margins. Gross body-energy headroom is explicitly
not treated as net spendable surplus. Mature recurring costs are limited to 10%
of the weakest observed gross headroom; immature combination states would be
limited to one quarter of that ceiling. D1-O consumes none of that recurring or
extra-development budget.

## Capability

Morphology gene 6 already exists in the fixed genome. It selects one of four
conservative offspring endowments: 0.75, 0.85, 0.95, or 1.05 energy. Their mean
is exactly the qualified fixed 0.9 endowment, and the largest event-debit
movement is 0.15. The fixed 0.1 event overhead and 0.8 retained parent reserve
remain unchanged.

The trait is independently functional and requires no gene combination. Its
minimum maturation window is one realized generation: direct consequences are
not assessed on the random founder state alone.

## Execution boundary

The environment, resource regeneration, harvest multipliers, maintenance,
founder count, initial energy, reproduction overhead, and parent reserve are
copied byte-for-byte from the qualified D1-N source config. Only the reproduction
schema and endowment-level vector may change. Independent source seeds
94101--94103 must pass the same v2 health contract before a shared-checkpoint
neutralization plan is authorized.

A passed source would authorize only the later design of a shared-checkpoint
direct-mechanism branch; v0.94 does not declare that command. Selection,
adaptation, niche, and long-horizon evolutionary claims require a later
independent panel with an explicitly post-maturation effect window.
