# D1-H demand-gated resource-sensing budget

## Charter role

D1-G proves that a fixed inherited extra-radius budget can be distributed over
several resource channels without changing its registered costs. Its three-seed
calibration remains negative in living count and mixed in mean energy. The
source protocol has no conservative per-channel internal stores, so static
affinity allocation cannot express which resource is currently missing.

D1-H composes two already objective world boundaries: inherited sensing reach
and conservative bounded resource storage. It does not add a reward, preferred
channel, role label, or result feedback.

## Capability contract

Morphology gene 7 still chooses total reach capacity from 1, 2, 4, and 8. Every
channel starts at radius one. The `capacity - 1` extra integer radius units are
Hamilton-apportioned using:

```text
inherited resource affinity × quantized open storage-room fraction
```

The product is re-normalized to the original fixed affinity budget before it is
used for both radius allocation and aggregate resource-gradient weighting. Thus
signal scale, total extra radius, genotype-derived maintenance/use/development
costs, and action vocabulary remain unchanged. If every store is full, the
allocation deterministically falls back to inherited affinity; the existing
room-gated local policy coordinate can still suppress resource demand.

## Calibration boundary

The first three-seed panel uses exact tick-480 shared checkpoints and a 120-tick
cost-preserving radius-one neutralization. It verifies demand quantization,
weight-budget closure, radius-budget closure, checkpoint identity, and
non-degenerate consequences. It has no pass/fail threshold and authorizes no
ecological, adaptive, coexistence, or selection claim.
