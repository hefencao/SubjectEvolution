# D1-H frozen pilot result

The three exact tick-480 shared-checkpoint pairs satisfy checkpoint identity,
paired randomness, genotype preservation, resource-field preservation, exact
extra-radius closure, observation-weight closure, and unchanged registered
sensing costs.

Demand gating changes the expressed channel radii, but the source populations
retain about 3.6 open storage channels per entity. Paired living-count, birth,
and death differences are exactly zero in all three seeds. Mean-energy
differences are -0.016338, +0.001318, and -0.002991.

This freezes D1-H as a valid but nearly consequence-neutral substrate
calibration. It does not authorize an ecological or selection claim. The result
indicates that synchronously open stores do not provide enough independent
internal demand structure for further sensing-allocation revisions.
