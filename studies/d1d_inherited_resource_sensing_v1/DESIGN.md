# D1-D inherited resource-sensing scale

## Charter role

The project charter prioritizes effective environmental diversity and inherited
body/capacity differentiation before social controllers. D1-D extends an
existing physical observation port rather than adding a reward, role label,
action, resource, or protected population.

## Capacity

Morphology coordinate 7 selects one of four periodic resource-gradient radii:
1, 2, 4, or 8 grid cells. Radius changes only the scale of the existing
resource-utility gradient. A broad radius bridges larger spatial structure but
loses fine local variation; radius one preserves the historical local central
difference.

## Costs

Every expressed radius pays separate maintenance and use energy each tick.
Newborns also pay a development cost. Costs increase linearly with radius and
remain charged in the phenotype-neutral branch.

## Shared-checkpoint neutralization

`neutralize-resource-sensing-radius` fixes only the effective observation
radius at one. It preserves genotype, inherited radius, resource fields,
maintenance/use/development costs, actions, and keyed randomness. Source and
neutral branches begin from the same content-locked tick-480 checkpoint and run
for 120 ticks.

## Interpretation boundary

The first run is substrate calibration. It checks that inherited scale reaches
world interaction and that the cost-preserving neutralization is executable.
It has no practical-effect threshold and cannot establish adaptation,
coexistence, ecological niches, or long-horizon selection. Those require a
later preregistered ecological design after calibration data are inspected.
