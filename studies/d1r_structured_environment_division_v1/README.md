# D1-R structured environment and within-group division

D1-Q did not produce a passing formal seed set. Its single qualified pilot is
therefore retained only as a source-code/configuration baseline; no demographic
or genetic authorization is inherited by D1-R.

D1-R pauses all single-round and formal gene-retention audits. The active task is
to build an environment in which a complete subject cannot satisfy every
physical demand in place and in isolation. Four raw-resource source provinces
are spatially separated, processing provinces are displaced from source
provinces, every body-producing recipe requires at least two raw channels, and
SHARE can transfer bounded raw material according only to donor surplus and
receiver room. No profession, group role, genotype reward or protected class is
introduced.

A group label alone is below the environment threshold. Formal maturity requires
three independent viable runs with replicated physical heterogeneity, real
within-group raw exchange, and at least two persistent division-candidate groups
in every seed. The diagnostic is observational and never feeds back into action,
fitness, grouping or reproduction.

The only single-seed step is `environment-probe`. It exists exclusively for
short parameter debugging. `probe-summary` always reports parameter-debug
status and cannot authorize a gene audit. The formal workflow is
`structured-panel` followed by `structure-summary`; even a passing environment
summary still does not authorize gene analysis in this version.
