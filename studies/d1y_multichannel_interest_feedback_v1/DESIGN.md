# D1-Y design boundary

The D1-X formula `received / (given + received)` remains only the material-return observation for one short window. It no longer defines complete interest.

A separate long-horizon knowledge channel is emitted only when a transferred copy is later matched to the receiver's executed context-action and checked against the realized five-dimensional consequence vector. Prediction error is normalized by the existing physical outcome scales, retained as signed quality in `[-1, 1]`, and weighted by the receiver's local confidence in that copy. Material and knowledge channels settle independently with separate windows and learning rates.

This is observational credit, not causal attribution. Shared-checkpoint knowledge neutralization remains mandatory before an Epoch 1 claim. The current row-addressed relation store also cannot keep actionable ties to a source entity after that source dies; such evidence is counted as orphaned rather than attached to a recycled row.
