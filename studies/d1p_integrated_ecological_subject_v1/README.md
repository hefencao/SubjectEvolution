# D1-P integrated ecological subject and persistence panel

D1-P corrects the previous iteration granularity. The qualified D1-O source is
already a plural subject with 704 inherited coordinates and a plural physical
environment. D1-P does not attach another isolated capability. It freezes and
runs the whole system as one baseline, then asks which inherited coordinates or
blocks remain present, concentrate, thin, or disappear across independent seeds.

The panel is not a 704-way causal audit. A flagged coordinate only becomes a
candidate for later adjustment when cross-seed genetic thinning is accompanied
by low expression/use evidence or a shared physical mismatch. Unflagged genes
are not declared beneficial; flagged genes are not declared harmful.
