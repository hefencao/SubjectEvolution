## What the paired run established

Across seeds `48001`, `48002`, and `48003`, the active branch used inherited feed-forward coupling at all three downstream hierarchy levels. The coupling-neutral branch retained the same v2 genes, mutation process, and structure cost but had exactly zero mediated signal.

## What it did not establish

The realized resource-affinity and functional-harvest-preference effective dimensions were lower in the active branch in all three seeds. Coupling added hierarchy and joint dependence, but the hierarchy was absorbed into the same four-channel harvest output vocabulary. It did not create a new functional axis.

Alive-count effects changed sign across seeds. They are not interpreted as a general benefit or cost. The run did not establish ecological differentiation, stable coexistence, or a reason to change module copy number.

## Structural decision

Retain the v2 compositional graph. Expand the versioned physical output basis before returning to environment-matching or module-copy experiments. The minimal extension must:

- expose physically different consequences rather than additional names for harvest routing;
- retain explicit maintenance and use costs;
- support output-only neutralization while genes and structural cost remain present;
- preserve the archived v1/v2 paths when disabled;
- avoid predefined ecological roles or diversity protection.
