# Assessment of the supplied D2-B 120/300-tick audits

## Input

The supplied archive contains six shared-checkpoint conditions:

- seeds 10001, 10002 and 10003;
- peak and trough checkpoints;
- 120-tick and 300-tick leave-one-module-out branches.

The results use `d2-module-leave-one-out-results-v1`, so they do not contain the
new immediate checkpoint footprint.

## Was running 300 ticks justified?

Yes. The 120-tick screen already contained practical effects in multiple
checkpoint conditions. Under the v0.41 standard, the correct automatic
recommendation would have been:

```text
run-300-tick-confirmation
```

Running 300 directly therefore did not waste the experiment.

## 300-tick findings

The strongest repeated mechanistic results are:

- total module expression increases extraction efficiency in five of six
  practical conditions and is positive in all six raw contrasts;
- module 2 increases extraction efficiency in four of six practical conditions;
- module 3 increases extraction efficiency in four of six practical conditions;
- total expression and modules 0, 1 and 3 repeatedly reduce functional
  harvest-preference dimensions.

Repeated ecological contrasts also exist, but they are not uniformly beneficial:

- total expression is associated with lower alive count in four of six
  conditions, higher environment dimensions in four of six, and lower effective
  transferred roots in four of six;
- module 1 repeatedly reduces effective transferred roots;
- modules 2 and 3 repeatedly reduce alive count while often increasing
  extraction efficiency or resource dimensions;
- module 0 is primarily path- or context-dependent.

This pattern is consistent with real functional trade-offs, redundancy and
trajectory amplification. It is not consistent with a simple claim that one
module is universally beneficial.

## Why duplication remains blocked

The source checkpoints have a median effective lineage count of approximately
`2.03`, with a minimum near `1.57`. The results also lack an immediate
lineage-resolved footprint because they were generated by v0.40.

Consequently the current result is:

```text
refresh-immediate-footprints-before-duplication-decision
```

Even after refreshing footprints, the dominant-lineage guard is expected to
remain active. D2-A module copy number, deletion and arbitrary routing therefore
remain blocked in v0.41.
