# D3-F inventory-conditioned processing-response audit

## Evidence gate

The supplied D3-E panel satisfies the substrate checks in all three shared-checkpoint pairs: support is nonuniform in active branches, neutral support is exactly one, costs occur in every branch, and both external ledgers close. Active-minus-neutral conversion is negative in all seeds, while survival differences have mixed signs. The next unresolved question is therefore a mediator question: do ordinary movements change exposure in relation to held inventory and processing opportunity?

## No new ecological mechanism

D3-F adds no policy feature, sensor, reward, controller, material flux, resource role, ecological role, or diversity protection. The existing action policy remains authoritative. The only new intervention is a counterfactual orientation control.

## Branch contract

Every seed writes one tick-0 full-world checkpoint and restores:

1. `original-support`: configured support surface;
2. `reversed-support`: the support surface rotated 180 degrees;
3. `neutral-support`: effective multiplier fixed at `1.0`.

The reversal changes only the non-material processing-support surface. It preserves resource fields, residue, renewal targets, genotype, inheritance, random state, processing-energy rates, and all other world mechanisms. The neutral branch also preserves execution cost.

## Read-only response observer

The observer runs after every authoritative step and never mutates simulation state. For entities present before and after the step, it reconstructs the pre-step raw-store demand and records:

- inventory-weighted support exposure;
- the next-tick support at the realized destination;
- the next-tick support at the no-move counterfactual position;
- destination-minus-stay support gain;
- movement alignment with the local inventory-weighted support gradient;
- separate summaries for resource movement and social/flee movement;
- channel-wise store-support occupancy correlation at scheduled snapshots.

The smooth support sample is a bilinear diagnostic view of the authoritative cell field. It does not replace the cell-sampled multiplier used by metabolism.

## Interpretation boundary

Orientation and neutralization differences are causal only with respect to those registered interventions under the shared checkpoint contract. Movement alignment is a measured mediator. A small positive or negative sign does not prove migration, specialization, coexistence, ecotypes, or named roles. Later migration experiments require repeated orientation-aligned response across seeds, horizons, and map scales before adding any sensor or control capability.
