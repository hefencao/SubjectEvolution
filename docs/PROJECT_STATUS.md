## v0.36 D1-A

Implemented schema:

```text
inherited-elastic-capacities-v1
```

Four independent inherited effective capacities now control existing mechanisms:

- working-memory dimensions;
- knowledge-storage bytes;
- relationship slots;
- incoming knowledge-attention slots.

All arrays retain fixed physical maxima. Capacity genes are appended after all existing genome regions. Each expressed capacity pays structural maintenance and birth development energy; existing mechanisms retain their use costs.

Causal intervention:

```text
neutralize-elastic-capacities
```

It fixes expression at midpoint discrete levels without changing genotype or mutation, applies to future offspring, trims state immediately and is checkpointed.

## Current scientific boundary

- D0 environmental field dimensionality is not proof of biological niches.
- D1-A capacity variation is not proof of functional specialization.
- The current implementation does not yet evolve sensor range, body storage, module count or structural gene duplication.
- Capacity–outcome correlations are observational until matched expression interventions repeat across seeds and phases.
- Social succession remains a group-label-dependent diagnostic, not proof of autonomous higher-level subjecthood.
