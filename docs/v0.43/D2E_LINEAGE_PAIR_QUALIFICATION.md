# D2-E lineage-pair qualification

D2-E converts raw D2-D checkpoint-lineage contrasts into a guarded continuation decision.

## Evidence layers

1. Exact non-zero divergence is recorded but is not sufficient.
2. Practical thresholds are applied to routed-output, retained-cost and total-expression effects separately.
3. Only routed-output effects can pass the continuation gate.
4. The same material direction must occur in at least two seeds and two non-dominant lineage identities.
5. A 300-tick confirmation must preserve every preselected checkpoint-lineage pair for each selected module.

The assessor never selects individual pairs by response magnitude. It may select a module after the module-level screen, but all dominant and non-dominant pairs already present in the 120-tick design remain in the confirmation plan.

## Supplied 120-tick result

- Module 2: repeated negative effect on `evolution.knowledge_effective_transferred_roots` in two seeds and two non-dominant lineage identities.
- Module 3: repeated negative effect on `target_lineage.alive` in two seeds and two non-dominant lineage identities, together with repeated positive effect on `target_lineage.mean_energy` in three seeds and three non-dominant lineage identities.
- No positive ecological routed-output effect is established at 120 ticks.
- Median effective lineages are about 2.27 and the lineage guard still fails.

Both modules qualify for a 300-tick causal-persistence check. Neither qualifies for duplication.
