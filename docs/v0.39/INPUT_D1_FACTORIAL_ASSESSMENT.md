# Input D1 affinity × capacity factorial assessment

## Scope

The supplied result contains six shared-checkpoint comparisons: three seeds,
each at peak and trough, with a 120-tick post-intervention horizon. Genotype,
stable IDs, source checkpoint and keyed randomness are held constant.

## Affinity expression

| Outcome | Six effects | Mean | Sign consistency |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Alive | +57, +59, +143, +146, +58, +80 | +90.50 | 6/6 positive |
| Mean energy | all positive | +0.1211 | 6/6 positive |
| Environment resource dimensions | +0.1125, +0.2465, +0.2253, +0.1224, +0.0541, +0.0516 | +0.1354 | 6/6 positive |
| Effective transferred roots | all positive | +27.33 | 6/6 positive |
| Extraction efficiency | all negative | -0.01455 | 6/6 negative |

This is the key result. Expressed affinity improves survival, energy, resource
dimensionality and cultural-root persistence even though immediate
actual/request extraction efficiency is lower. The effect is therefore not a
free increase in gross harvest efficiency.

Resource-affinity effective dimensions are slightly lower in all six expressed
branches. This may indicate selection/concentration toward useful affinities,
but the local comparison does not identify why.

## Capacity expression

Capacity expression is not directionally stable:

- alive: `+7, -2, -1, +1, +49, +31`;
- mean energy: negative in four of six;
- resource dimensions: negative in five of six;
- effective transferred roots: negative in the first seed, strongly positive in
  the second, positive in the third;
- capacity effective dimensions remain strongly higher by construction because
  the neutral branch fixes all capacities to the midpoint.

The mean alive effect (`+14.17`) is driven mainly by seed 10003 and cannot be
used as a general capacity benefit. Capacity expression should remain an
independent D1 axis with bound/cost sensitivity, rather than being folded into a
single “complexity” score.

## Interaction

Affinity × capacity interaction is mixed. Alive interaction has mean `-0.5`
with four negative and two positive checkpoints; environment and cultural-root
interactions are mostly negative. The factors are not simply additive, but no
cross-seed universal interaction direction is established.

## Decision

The affinity result passes the minimum gate for a bounded D2-A test because it
is repeated across all six matched comparisons and is not explained by higher
immediate extraction efficiency.

It does **not** justify:

- arbitrary action outputs;
- module duplication or deletion;
- new physical effectors;
- dynamic code generation;
- a claim that affinity expression is universally necessary.

D2-A is therefore restricted to contextual residuals over the already validated
four harvest request ports. Capacity remains under D1 paired investigation.
