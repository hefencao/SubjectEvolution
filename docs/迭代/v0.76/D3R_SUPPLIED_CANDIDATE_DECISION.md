# D3-R supplied candidate decision

Candidate: `functional-regulatory-oxygen-uptake-acute-effect-v1`

All eight fixed-checkpoint seed panels are eligible and all eight satisfy the direct regulatory-output manipulation contract. Six seed effects decrease cumulative realized oxygen uptake and two increase it, so the direction-consistency gate is met at 0.75.

The equal-seed median relative effect is approximately `-0.00010983`, or a decrease of about 0.011%. This is far below the preregistered 2% practical-effect threshold. D3-R therefore stops before replication as a manipulation-confirmed promotion-negative candidate.

This result bounds the acute physiology-output path under the declared checkpoint, horizon, metric and intervention. It does not establish a universal zero effect for functional modules, and it does not support organ, niche or evolutionary-selection claims.
